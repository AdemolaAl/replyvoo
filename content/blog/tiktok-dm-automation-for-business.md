## Why TikTok DMs are different

On most platforms, interest builds slowly. On TikTok it arrives in spikes. A video can sit quietly for two days and then explode overnight. That has three consequences:

- **Volume is unpredictable.** You can't staff for a spike you can't see coming.
- **Interest fades fast.** A viewer who DMs you is excited now. Tomorrow they've watched 300 more videos.
- **The audience is wide.** A viral video can bring messages from many countries and in many languages.

A human team can't absorb that. An AI agent can, because it handles every conversation at once, at any hour, in the language each person writes in.

## How TikTok DM automation works: TikTok Business Messaging

TikTok offers an official **Business Messaging API** that lets approved software read and reply to a business account's direct messages. That's the only safe foundation for automation. Tools that log in to the app as you or script the mobile app put your account at risk; avoid them.

Through the API, an AI agent can:

- Receive new direct messages as they arrive
- Reply with text and links
- Handle conversations started from TikTok ads that point people to your DMs
- Pass a conversation to a person on your team

## Who can use it

TikTok sets clear limits on who can use Business Messaging. Check these before you plan around it.

**Business Accounts only.** Your TikTok account must be a Business Account. You can switch in TikTok's account settings.

**Supported regions only.** TikTok doesn't offer Business Messaging everywhere. At the time of writing, accounts registered in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are excluded. The list of supported regions can change, so confirm your eligibility before building a campaign around it.

**Approved partners only.** TikTok grants API access to software partners after an application and review. Approval timing isn't something any vendor controls, which is why Replyvoo's TikTok channel is marked coming soon and why we don't promise a date.

**Younger users.** TikTok limits direct messaging for younger users, so some viewers can't message any account. Plan other ways for them to reach you, like a link in bio.

## The rules: a reply-only channel with a window

TikTok Business Messaging is built for **replies**, not outreach.

- **Businesses can't start conversations.** You can only message people who messaged you first.
- **There's a reply window.** After the customer's last message, your business has a limited time to reply (48 hours at the time of writing), with a cap on how many messages you can send in that window.
- **No broadcasts.** You can't message everyone who ever DMed you with an offer.

For a business, the takeaway is simple: **the first reply matters more on TikTok than anywhere else.** You get one window, it closes, and you can't chase people later. Answering in seconds instead of hours is the whole game.

## Leads from TikTok ads

TikTok lets advertisers run ads that send people into a direct message conversation with the business. For lead generation, that's powerful: instead of filling a form and waiting for a call, the person is already chatting with you.

But it only works if someone answers. Paying for clicks into a DM inbox nobody's watching is one of the most expensive mistakes in paid social.

A good setup for ad leads looks like this:

1. **The ad promises one clear thing.** "DM us to get this week's price" beats "Contact us".
2. **The AI greets the lead instantly** and confirms what they came for.
3. **It asks two or three qualifying questions.** For a fitness coach: goal, experience, home or gym. For a store: size, colour, delivery city.
4. **It sends the right next step.** A checkout link, a booking link or a programme page.
5. **Hot leads go to a person** when a human touch closes better, such as high-ticket offers or custom orders.
6. **Every lead is scored and saved**, so you can see which ads bring buyers, not just messages.

## What to prepare

- **A Business Account** in a supported region.
- **Your offer, in plain words.** Products or programmes, prices, what's included, delivery or access details.
- **Qualifying questions** that tell you who's a real buyer.
- **Links** for checkout, booking or sign-up that work well on mobile.
- **Answers to the top 15 questions** from your comments. Your comments section is a free list of what people want to know.
- **Handoff rules** for brand deals, complaints and custom requests.
- **Tone.** TikTok is casual. Your AI agent should sound like your videos, not like a bank.

## Measuring results

- **First reply time.** It should be seconds, especially on viral days.
- **Replies inside the window.** Every DM answered before the window closes.
- **Link clicks per conversation.** How often the chat reaches checkout or booking.
- **Sales by source.** Organic video vs ad, so you know where to spend.

## Common mistakes

- **Running DM ads with nobody watching.** You pay for conversations that die unanswered.
- **Long, robotic first replies.** Keep the first message short and human, then ask one question.
- **Asking too many questions.** Two or three is plenty before you offer the next step.
- **Ignoring region limits.** If your account isn't eligible, plan your funnel around another channel.
