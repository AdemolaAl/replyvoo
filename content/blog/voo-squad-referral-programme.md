If you like Replyvoo, chances are you know other business owners who are drowning in chats too. **Voo Squad** is our referral programme. You share Replyvoo, they get AI Closers, and you earn a recurring commission every month they stay on a paid plan.

This guide covers how it works, the commission tiers, how payouts work and how to refer in a way that actually helps people.

![Voo Squad dashboard with referral link, tier progress and earnings](/img/screens/squad.png)

## How Voo Squad works

1. **Get your link.** Open **Voo Squad** in the dashboard and copy your personal referral link.
2. **Share it.** With customers, clients, friends in business, your audience.
3. **They sign up** through your link and start on any plan.
4. **They upgrade to a paid plan.** That's a paying referral.
5. **You earn commission** on what they pay, every month they stay subscribed.
6. **Get paid monthly** as Voo Credits or cash.

Simple, recurring and tied to real value delivered.

## Commission tiers

Your commission rate grows as you bring in more paying referrals:

- **20%** from your first paying referral
- **25%** once you reach **10 paying referrals**
- **30%** once you reach **50 paying referrals**

The commission is recurring. As long as your referrals keep paying, you keep earning.

> Recommend something you genuinely use, and the commission becomes a bonus, not a pitch.

## A worked example

Imagine a hypothetical marketing consultant in Nairobi who sets up Telegram sales for small brands.

- She refers 4 clients to **Starter** ($29/month) and 6 to **Pro** ($149/month).
- That's 10 paying referrals, which moves her to the 25% tier.
- Monthly referral revenue: (4 × $29) + (6 × $149) = $116 + $894 = $1,010.
- Her monthly commission at 25%: **$92.50**.

If her clients stay on those plans, that commission repeats every month. As she adds more clients, both her base and her rate grow.

Your numbers will depend on who you refer and which plans they choose. This is just to show how the maths works.

## Credits or cash

Payouts are monthly, and you choose:

- **Voo Credits.** Added straight to your **Voo Wallet**. Use them for extra conversations, voice replies, Pro Brain, Blasts or extra Closers. Great if you run Replyvoo for your own business. See [how credits work](/blog/replyvoo-pricing-conversations-and-credits).
- **Cash.** Withdraw by bank transfer, mobile money or USDT once your balance reaches **$300**. Below $300, it simply carries over to next month.

A practical approach: take credits while you're growing your own usage, then switch to cash once your referral income outgrows what you spend.

## How to refer well

The best referrals come from showing, not selling.

**Show your own results.** Share a screenshot of your Closer handling a real question (remove customer details first). Show the Live Room. People trust what they can see.

**Teach the setup.** Write or record a short tutorial on connecting a Telegram bot or building a Closer. Link our guides, like [Getting started with Replyvoo](/blog/getting-started-with-replyvoo) and [Build your first Closer in Agent Studio](/blog/build-your-first-closer-agent-studio), with your referral link.

**Start them on the right plan.** Spark is free. Let people test with real customers, then upgrade when they see the value. A referral who stays for a year is worth more than one who upgrades too early and churns.

**Help them get the first win.** Offer to review their knowledge base or Closer settings. Their success is your recurring income.

**Be honest.** Say you earn a commission. It builds trust and it's the right thing to do.

## What's not allowed

To keep Voo Squad fair for everyone:

- **No spam.** Don't mass-message strangers, post your link in groups where it isn't welcome, or Blast it to people who didn't ask.
- **No self-referrals** or fake accounts.
- **No misleading claims.** Don't promise results, earnings or features that aren't real. Don't say WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok are live until they are. See [what's coming soon](/blog/whatsapp-instagram-tiktok-coming-soon).
- **Follow our [acceptable use policy](/legal/acceptable-use).**

Breaking these rules can put your commission and your place in the programme at risk.

## Frequently asked questions

**When do I get paid?** Monthly.

**Do free Spark users count?** They count as sign-ups. They become paying referrals when they upgrade to a paid plan.
