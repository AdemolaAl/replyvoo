Many sellers don't run a bot. Customers just message *them*, on their personal Telegram, because that's the number on the flyer, the handle in the bio, the contact a friend passed along. Those chats pile up between your family group and your cousin's memes, and replies slip.

Telegram Business lets you connect a chatbot to your own account so it can reply on your behalf. Replyvoo uses this to put an AI Closer on your personal Telegram. Customers keep messaging the same account they always have. They just get answers faster.

## What you need

- **Telegram Premium** on the account you want to automate. Telegram Business features are part of Premium.
- **A Replyvoo account.** The free Spark plan works. Start at [/app](/app).
- **A Closer** ready in Agent Studio, with a solid knowledge base.
- The latest version of the Telegram app.

## How it works

When you connect a chatbot through Telegram Business, Telegram passes messages from the chats you choose to that bot, and the bot can reply *as you*. The customer sees the reply in their chat with you, from your name and photo.

You decide which chats the Closer can touch. You can exclude contacts, include only new chats, or pick specific people. Your private conversations stay private.

> The best automation is invisible to the customer and fully visible to you.

Every chat the Closer handles still shows up in **Pulse Inbox** in Replyvoo, so you can watch, correct and take over at any time.

## Step 1: Prepare your Closer first

Don't connect anything until your Closer is ready. On your personal account, a bad reply feels like *you* said it.

1. Open **Agent Studio** in Replyvoo.
2. Pick a job. For a personal account selling products or services, **Ace (Sales Closer)** is a good fit. If you mostly handle bookings, use **Tempo (Booking Pro)**. For high-value clients, **Royal (VIP Concierge)**.
3. Set personality close to how you actually write. If you're casual and use emoji, match that. If you're brief, slide toward Short.
4. Load your knowledge base: prices, delivery, payment methods, policies, hours.
5. Turn on **Smart handoff** so the Closer flags anything it shouldn't answer.
6. Run **Test chat** with ten real questions you've received this month.

## Step 3: Connect the chatbot in Telegram

On your phone:

1. Open Telegram and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Telegram Business**.
3. Tap **Chatbots**.
4. Enter the bot username Replyvoo gave you and select it.
5. Choose which chats the bot can access (more on this below).
6. Make sure the bot is allowed to **reply to messages**.
7. Save.

That's it. Send a message to your account from another Telegram account to test it.

## Step 4: Choose the right chats

This is the most important decision on this page. Telegram lets you set access in a few ways, and the wording in the app may change slightly between versions, but the idea is the same:

- **All private chats except selected.** The Closer handles everyone, and you exclude the people it shouldn't touch.
- **Only selected chats.** The Closer handles only the chats or chat types you pick.

You can typically include or exclude groups of chats such as **Contacts**, **Non-contacts**, **New chats** and **Existing chats**.

A setup that works for most sellers:

- **Include:** Non-contacts and new chats. These are mostly customers and leads.
- **Exclude:** Contacts. Your family, friends and colleagues keep talking to the real you.
- **Exclude individually:** suppliers, partners and anyone you always want to answer personally.

If your contacts list is full of customers, flip it: include everyone, then exclude the specific personal chats you care about.

## Best practices for personal accounts

**Sound like you.** Customers know your style. Write a short "about me" in the knowledge base: how you greet people, how you sign off, words you use.

**Be honest when it matters.** If a customer asks "Am I talking to a bot?", your Closer should answer honestly. Put that in its instructions.

**Keep sensitive topics human.** Refunds over a certain amount, disputes, complaints about staff. Add them to Smart handoff rules so they come to you.

**Don't let it promise things you can't deliver.** Spell out delivery times and stock rules clearly. "We deliver within Lagos in 1 to 3 working days" is far better than "fast delivery".

**Check the Night Shift Report every morning.** It summarises what happened while you slept, including chats that need you.

## Personal account or bot: which should you use?

Use **personal Telegram Business** when:

- Customers already have your personal handle
- You're a solo seller, consultant, coach or service provider
- Your brand *is* you

Use a **bot** when:

- You want a separate, shareable sales channel
- You run a team and don't want everything flowing through one person
- You want to run a Closer in groups
- You don't have Telegram Premium

Many businesses use both: a bot for ads and public links, and the personal account for repeat clients. Each can have its own Closer. Setting up a bot is covered in [Connect a Telegram bot with BotFather](/blog/connect-telegram-bot-botfather).

## Troubleshooting

**No Telegram Business option in Settings.** Check that Premium is active and your app is up to date.

**The bot isn't replying.** Confirm the chat type is included in your Chatbots access settings, that the bot can reply, and that AI replies is on for that chat in Pulse Inbox.

**It replied to a friend.** Add them to the excluded list, or exclude Contacts entirely.

**Replies stopped mid-month.** You may have used your plan's conversations. Check the Command Center and the Voo Wallet. See [Replyvoo pricing explained](/blog/replyvoo-pricing-conversations-and-credits).

**You want to pause everything.** In Telegram, go to Settings → Telegram Business → Chatbots and pause or remove the bot. You can reconnect anytime.
